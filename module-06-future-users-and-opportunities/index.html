    Module 6: Future Users and Opportunities

    6.1 Emerging User Segments

    Retail Investors represent a massive untapped potential in the tokenized asset space. Historically, asset classes like private equity and commercial real estate required high capital minimums, but tokenization enables fractional ownership, which radically lowers the barriers to entry for retail participants. Retail participation is scaling swiftly at a 50.20% compound annual growth rate, aided by compliant crowdfunding exemptions and intuitive mobile wallets that hide the underlying blockchain complexity. A prominent example of this expansion is Franklin Templeton, which launched a tokenized USD money market fund for professional investors in Hong Kong and has explicitly stated its goal to offer a retail-approved tokenized fund in the region, pending approval from the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC). Additionally, the firm has already secured regulatory approval to launch its first retail tokenized fund in Singapore in 2025.

    Pension Funds and Endowments manage the largest pools of capital among institutional investors, yet they currently exhibit the lowest rates of digital asset adoption. Surveys indicate that these conservative investor segments have little to no exposure to digital assets, frequently citing concerns regarding price volatility, security, regulatory classification, and environmental impacts. However, these long-term investors are positioned for significant future adoption as market infrastructure matures and regulatory clarity improves. For instance, pension funds increasingly view tokenized real estate and infrastructure as highly suitable matches for their long-duration liabilities, particularly given the potential for enhanced liquidity. To satisfy fiduciary duties, some pension plans are already employing multi-custodian structures to securely manage digital assets and eliminate single points of failure.

    Corporate Treasuries are emerging as a major user segment by leveraging tokenized money market funds for yield-generating cash management and intraday liquidity. Tokenization allows corporate treasurers to optimize their cash management strategies by providing 24/7 liquidity, instant settlement, and the ability to earn yield every calendar day. Through programmable smart contracts, treasurers can automate complex, labor-intensive operations. This programmable money enables cash to automatically move between operational accounts and yield-generating positions based on real-time liquidity needs, turning multi-day tracking processes into near-instantaneous, automated actions.

    Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) are using tokenization to unlock new avenues for capital access, bypassing traditional funding bottlenecks. Historically excluded from traditional private equity and debt markets, SMEs can now utilize Security Token Offerings (STOs) as a regulated, cost-effective alternative to traditional IPOs or venture capital rounds. Tokenization is also revolutionizing private credit by allowing SMEs to tokenize their invoices and accounts receivable. Platforms like Centrifuge enable businesses to convert these illiquid assets into tokenized pools, providing them with rapid financing from a global investor base without relying on expensive, traditional banking credit facilities.

    Emerging Market Investors are utilizing tokenization as a critical tool to leapfrog legacy financial systems and access global capital markets. In emerging economies where traditional capital markets and financial infrastructures are less developed, tokenization democratizes investment opportunities. With just a smartphone and internet access, individuals can bypass traditional geographic limitations and intermediaries to invest in global financial assets. By lowering minimum investment thresholds and reducing currency conversion friction, tokenization allows emerging market investors to benefit from fractional ownership and improved liquidity across equities, fixed income, and alternative assets.

    6.2 New Use Cases

    The rapid advancement of real-world asset (RWA) tokenization is moving well beyond standard securities to unlock entirely new economic models and efficiencies. Here is an expansion of these emerging use cases:

    Cross-Border Investment

    Tokenization fundamentally simplifies cross-border investments by removing geographic barriers, reducing currency conversion friction, and enabling seamless, real-time international settlement. Because blockchain ledgers operate 24/7 and without borders, businesses in emerging marketssuch as a company in Brazilcan easily raise capital from investors in Europe or Asia without being hindered by traditional logistical and regulatory roadblocks. This borderless nature expands global market access, diversifies investor portfolios, and democratizes investment in traditionally illiquid assets.

    Collateral Mobility

    Tokenizing assets like money market funds (MMFs) and U.S. Treasuries allows them to be pledged, transferred, and reused as collateral instantly without the constraints of traditional settlement delays or the need to move assets across underlying legacy ledgers. A prime example of this is BlackRocks USD Institutional Digital Liquidity Fund (BUIDL). BUIDL tokens are increasingly used as institutional collateral across digital asset platforms, including major crypto exchanges like Binance, Crypto.com, and Deribit. Institutional traders can post BUIDL tokens as margin for leveraged trades, allowing them to simultaneously maintain their trading positions while continuing to earn yield on the underlying U.S. Treasury assets.

    DeFi Integration

    Tokenization acts as a critical bridge between traditional finance (TradFi) and decentralized finance (DeFi). By tokenizing physical and financial assets, institutions can bring tangible, stable value into the DeFi ecosystem to be used for lending, borrowing, yield optimization, and liquidity provision. For example, MakerDAO accepts various RWAs to collateralize its DAI stablecoin. Similarly, Ondo Finance leverages BUIDL tokens as the underlying asset for its OUSG and USDY products, channeling institutional-grade treasury yields directly into DeFi protocols.

    Supply Chain Finance (Tokenized Invoices)

    For small and medium enterprises (SMEs), cash flow is often tied up in accounts receivable and unpaid invoices. Supply chain tokenization turns these invoices into digital tokens that can be sold or used as collateral on lending platforms to secure immediate cash. Platforms like Centrifuge enable businesses to pool these tokenized invoices and connect them to DeFi liquidity pools. In this model, DeFi investors earn yield generated from real-world economic activity, while the blockchain's immutable ledger prevents fraud, such as double-financing the same invoice.

    ESG and Carbon Credits

    Traditional voluntary carbon markets have long suffered from opaque tracking, fragmented registries, and the double-counting of emission reductions. Tokenizing carbon credits provides a reliable, transparent, and auditable mechanism for environmental, social, and governance (ESG) assets. Each tokenized credit is uniquely identifiable on a public ledger, ensuring its authenticity. Smart contracts automate the retirement of the token the moment a credit is redeemed, securing the integrity of offsets and preventing the same reduction from being sold twice. Projects like Toucan Protocol and KlimaDAO, as well as institutional pilots by J.P. Morgan and S&P Global, are actively establishing this liquid, trust-minimized carbon market.

    Intellectual Property (IP) Tokenization

    Traditional intellectual property management involves exorbitant costs, low transparency, and difficult cross-border enforcement. By tokenizing IPsuch as music royalties, film residuals, software licenses, patents, and trademarkscreators and businesses can sell fractional stakes in their future income streams to a global audience. This allows creators to monetize their work and access capital today without surrendering total ownership. Furthermore, smart contracts can automate the execution of licensing agreements and ensure instant, transparent royalty distribution directly to token holders.

    Executive Refresher

    Here is an executive refresher summary of the future users and opportunities in asset tokenization:

    1. The Untapped Retail Market via Fractionalization

    Tokenization allows high-value, historically illiquid assets (like commercial real estate, fine art, and private equity) to be divided into smaller digital units. By drastically lowering minimum investment thresholds, tokenization democratizes access and opens up massive new capital pools from retail investors who were previously priced out of these exclusive asset classes.

    2. Institutional Migration to "On-Chain Yield"

    Tier-1 institutions, asset managers, and sovereign wealth funds are rapidly moving past experimental pilots into at-scale production. Instead of speculative crypto assets, they are flocking to tokenized U.S. Treasuries and Money Market Funds (MMFs) to achieve capital efficiency, 24/7 liquidity, and the predictable returns of traditional finance on blockchain rails.

    3. The $400 Billion Private Markets Goldmine

    Private markets (Private Equity, Venture Capital, Private Credit) represent the highest alpha opportunity for tokenization due to their inherent illiquidity and high administrative friction. Tokenization enables the fractionalization of Limited Partner (LP) interests and uses smart contracts to automate cumbersome processes like capital calls, potentially unlocking $400 billion in new annual revenue for the alternatives industry.

    4. Corporate Treasurers Driving Intraday Liquidity

    Corporate treasurers are emerging as key future users. By utilizing programmable smart contracts and tokenized deposits, treasurers can automate cash movements, manage internal cash pools for intraday liquidity, and execute near-instant cross-border settlements, freeing up capital that would otherwise be trapped in lengthy T+2 settlement cycles.

    5. Pension Funds and Endowments on the Horizon

    Though currently showing lower adoption rates compared to other institutional investors, pension funds and endowments manage massive pools of capital and are prime future users. As regulatory clarity and institutional-grade infrastructure mature, these conservative investors are expected to heavily adopt tokenized real estate and infrastructure investments to match their long-duration liabilities with improved liquidity.

    6. Emergence of New Revenue Streams and Business Models

    Tokenization is creating entirely new revenue channels for financial institutions and service providers. Executives must recognize opportunities to monetize through digital asset custody, tokenized fund brokerage, infrastructure-as-a-service, micro-investing platforms, and the creation of hyper-personalized portfolios driven by smart contracts.

    7. The Convergence of TradFi and DeFi

    The firewall between Traditional Finance (TradFi) and Decentralized Finance (DeFi) is collapsing. In the near future, tokenized Real-World Assets (RWAs) will increasingly move seamlessly across chains to be used as collateral in DeFi protocols. This composability allows institutions to lend, borrow, and generate yield on tokenized assets in a borderless, 24/7 digital ecosystem.
